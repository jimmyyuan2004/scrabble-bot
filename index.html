How to play

Starting a game

Press New Game, pick your settings, and share the code or invite link. Friends enter the code on the home page. Once at least 2 players are in, anyone can press Start Game. Everyone gets 7 tiles and the turn order is drawn at random.

Making a play

  • Click a tile in your rack, then click an empty square.
  • Click a placed tile to take it back, or press Recall to take them all back.
  • Place tiles in one row or column with no gaps, then press Submit Play.
  • You can arrange tiles at any time, but you can only submit on your turn.

Blank tiles

A ? tile is a blank. When you place one, choose the letter it stands for. It's worth 0 points and shows a small * on the board.

Challenging

  • A submitted play is pending and has a dashed outline. The next player's clock starts straight away.
  • Challenge: all clocks pause for up to 2 minutes while you look the word up. The game doesn't check words, so agree on a dictionary. Then press Word is valid or Not a word.
  • Not a word: the tiles go back and that player has lost their turn.
  • Word is valid: the play stands. In a double challenge game, the challenger misses their next turn.
  • If nobody challenges, the play stands when the next player takes their turn, and that player draws new tiles.
  • Some games set a time to challenge, shown under the pending play. When it runs out, the play stands and the new tiles are drawn.

Time control

  • No limit: no clock. If someone is away, the others can press Skip next to their name.
  • Per turn: run out of time and you lose that turn.
  • Total time: everyone has their own clock for the whole game. It only runs on your turn. Past zero you lose points each minute, and if you go too far over, you forfeit.
  • Scores aren't tracked yet, so subtract any penalty shown next to a name yourselves.

Other controls

  • Exchange: swap tiles with the bag. This uses your turn.
  • Pass: skip your turn.
  • Undo: take back your own pending play and get your turn back.
  • ⋯ menu: copy the invite link, go back home, or end the game for everyone.

Game settings

You choose the time control and challenge rules when you create a game. For any questions about game settings, see the README.

Spectating

Enter a game code on the home page and choose Spectate to watch without joining. You don't need a name. Spectators see the board and everyone's racks, but can't change anything.

Keep in mind

The game doesn't keep score yet, so track that yourselves. It remembers you in this browser only, so stick to the same device for each game.
