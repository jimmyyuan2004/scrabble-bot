How to play

Starting a game

Press New Game and share the code or invite link. Friends enter the code on the home page. Once at least 2 players are in, anyone can press Start Game, and everyone gets 7 tiles.

Making a play

  • Click a tile in your rack, then click an empty square.
  • Click a placed tile to take it back, or press Recall to take them all back.
  • Place tiles in one row or column with no gaps, then press Submit Play.

Blank tiles

A ? tile is a blank. When you place one, choose the letter it stands for. It's worth 0 points and shows a small * on the board.

Accepting and challenging

  • A submitted play is pending and has a dashed outline until it's resolved.
  • Accept: the play stands and that player draws new tiles.
  • Challenge: the word is rejected and the tiles go back to that player's rack.
  • If the next player submits a play, earlier pending plays are accepted automatically.
  • The game doesn't check words. Agree on a dictionary and decide together.

Other controls

  • Exchange: swap tiles with the bag instead of playing.
  • Undo: take back your own pending play.
  • ⋯ menu: copy the invite link, go back home, or end the game for everyone.

Keep in mind

The game doesn't track turns or scores, so keep those yourselves. It remembers you in this browser only, so stick to the same device for each game.
